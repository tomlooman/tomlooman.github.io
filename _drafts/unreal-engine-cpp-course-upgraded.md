Five years after the original C++ course launched, I have rebuilt it from the ground up in Unreal Engine 5.6, supporting newer versions including 5.8, an entirely new curriculum, and more than 30 hours of newly recorded lessons.

In September 2021, I launched 'Professional Game Development in C++ and Unreal Engine', a project-based course built around everything I had learned from years of developing games and teaching Unreal Engine - it was originally taught at Stanford University. Exactly five years later, I have rebuilt the entire course for the latest versions of Unreal Engine 5. Every lesson has been re-recorded, the game project and code have been redesigned, and the curriculum has been expanded and reorganized around the way I teach professional Unreal Engine C++ today. It reflects current workflows, coding standards, and the practices I use in my own development work. 

Best of all, **the complete rebuild available at no additional cost for existing students!** [Jump straight into the new curriculum](https://courses.tomlooman.com/courses/game-development-in-cpp-unreal-engine-5/lectures/62227301)

## Five Year Anniversary

What began as a straightforward Unreal Engine 5 update gradually turned into something much larger. Over the past year, I have rebuilt the course project, redesigned its gameplay systems, expanded and reorganized the curriculum, and re-recorded every lesson.

Five years of student questions helped me recognize where concepts could be explained more clearly, where the learning path could flow better, and which subjects deserved more depth. I have also continued to learn through my own development work, and that experience has been fed directly back into the new curriculum.

The result is a densely packed 30-hour learning path built around modern Unreal Engine 5 development, with updated systems, clearer explanations, and considerably more advanced material than the original course.

**It launches publicly on September 21, exactly five years after the original.** [Explore the Rebuilt C++ Course](/courses/unrealengine-cpp/)

## More Than an Engine Upgrade

The rebuilt course is a densely packed 30-hour learning path created for modern Unreal Engine 5 C++ development.

It progresses from the essential Unreal C++ foundations into increasingly advanced gameplay systems. Along the way, I explain not only how each system works, but why it is designed that way, which alternatives exist, and how to diagnose problems when things do not work as expected. New lessons also introduce more advanced subjects such as data-oriented design.

The goal has always been not simply to finish the course project, but to understand Unreal Engine well enough to make sound engineering decisions and confidently build the next project independently. That goal has been reinforced in the re-recording as a fundamental goal and student feedback has been incredible.

The production has also been completely upgraded, with higher-quality video and audio, clearer presentation, and tighter editing to maintain a consistent pace throughout the course.

## Built to Last

Rather than continuing to patch the original UE4 course with compatibility notes and isolated update videos, I wanted to create one consistent learning experience from beginning to end.

This gave me the opportunity to replace outdated workflows, improve the progression of the curriculum, and introduce programming concepts that I believe are increasingly important in modern game development.

Unreal Engine will continue to evolve, but rebuilding the course around current engine versions and lasting programming principles gives it a much stronger foundation for the years ahead.

Existing students are welcome to follow the new course again. Even if you completed the original version, you will find redesigned systems, updated workflows, and entirely new lessons throughout the rebuilt curriculum.

## What's Next? Multiplayer Programming

With the rebuilt core course complete, my next focus is a dedicated Multiplayer Programming module covering Unreal Engine replication, Iris, multiplayer gameplay architecture, testing, and debugging.

Multiplayer was included in the original course, so **every existing student will receive the module at no additional cost.**

For the rebuilt edition, I separated multiplayer into its own advanced module. This creates a more focused core curriculum for developers who do not need multiplayer programming, while giving me room to explore modern Unreal Engine multiplayer development in greater depth. Even without multiplayer, the new 30-hour core curriculum is larger than the complete original course (at roughly 25 hours).

The module is planned for release later this year (Q4 2026). **During the fifth-anniversary launch (lasting till September 30th), new students will also receive it at no additional cost** and gain access automatically when it is released. 

After this sale, the module will be a separate add-on purchase.
