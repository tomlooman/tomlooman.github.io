Five years after the original C++ course launched, I have rebuilt it from the ground up in Unreal Engine 5.6, supporting all newer versions including 5.8, an entirely new curriculum, and more than 30 hours of newly recorded lessons.

In September 2021, I launched 'Professional Game Development in C++ and Unreal Engine', a project-based course built around everything I had learned from years of developing games and teaching Unreal Engine - it was originally taught at Stanford University. Exactly five years later, I have rebuilt the entire course for the latest versions of Unreal Engine 5. Every lesson has been re-recorded, the game project and code have been redesigned, and the curriculum has been expanded and reorganized around the way I teach professional Unreal Engine C++ today. Ensure it's based on the latest best practices and coding standards. And the best part, **it's available at no additional cost for existing students!**

## Five Year Anniversary

What began as a simple Unreal Engine 5 update gradually turned into something much larger.

Over the past year, I have rebuilt and re-recorded the entire course. The result is a new 30-hour learning path created around modern Unreal Engine 5 development, with updated systems, new lessons, better explanations, and more advanced material than the original course.

**It's now ready to launch, exactly 5 years after the original.**

## Built to Last

Instead of continuously patching the old (UE4) course forever, it was time to fully upgrade. This gave me the opportunity to remove any outdated principles, incorporate new best practices and concepts that I believe are critical to modern game development such as data-oriented design.

The new course launch will ensure the course is reliable and modern for multiple years. Current students are welcome to follow the new course again and see just how much has been changed for UE5 and discover new valuable tricks and information that I included in the updated version.

Providing this upgrade at no additional cost is an important part of my commitment to lifetime access. The students who supported the original course should continue to benefit as Unreal Engine changes and the curriculum improves. I promised this years ago, and here I am today delivering on that promise.

## What's Next? Multiplayer!

With the rebuilt core course complete, my next focus is a dedicated Multiplayer Programming module covering Unreal Engine replication, Iris, multiplayer gameplay architecture, testing, and debugging.

Multiplayer was included in the original course, so **every existing student will receive the new module at no additional cost.**

For the rebuilt edition, I separated multiplayer into its own advanced module. This creates a more focused 30-hour core curriculum for developers who do not need multiplayer programming, while giving me room to explore modern Unreal Engine multiplayer development in considerably greater depth.

The module is planned for release later this year. **During the fifth-anniversary launch, new students will also receive it at no additional cost** and gain access automatically when it is released.
